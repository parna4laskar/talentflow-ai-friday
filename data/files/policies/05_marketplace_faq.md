# Talent Marketplace FAQ (sample)

**How are matches calculated?**
Each match combines skill fit (35%), adjacent-skill potential (15%), availability (15%), location fit (10%), application interest (10%) and a predicted success probability from historical internal moves (15%).

**Will my manager know I applied?**
Not until the interview stage. You must tell your manager before your first interview.

**What if I am partially available?**
Partially available employees can be matched to roles that start after their current allocation drops, or to part-time assignments that fit their free capacity.

**How do I improve my match score?**
Keep your skills up to date, get skills verified, set your career interests and preferred locations, and complete the learning pathways recommended for your target roles.

**Can I see why I was not shortlisted?**
Yes. The Career Hub shows your skill gaps for each role and the learning pathway that would close them.
