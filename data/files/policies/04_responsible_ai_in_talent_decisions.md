# Responsible AI in Talent Decisions (sample standard)

1. **Human in the loop** – AI recommendations never make final selection decisions. Hiring managers approve or reject every shortlisted candidate and record a reason.
2. **Protected attributes** – Gender, age, ethnicity, religion, disability and family status are never used as matching features and are never sent to large language models.
3. **Fairness monitoring** – Each shortlist is checked with the four-fifths (80%) rule across gender, age band and region. If a group's selection rate falls below 80% of the highest group's rate, the shortlist is re-balanced with equally qualified candidates and the event is logged.
4. **Inclusive job descriptions** – Job descriptions are screened for exclusionary language (for example "young", "native speaker", "culture fit with our guys") before they are published.
5. **Explainability** – Every recommendation shows its score breakdown and the evidence behind it (skills, availability, location and interests).
6. **Privacy** – Personal data such as emails and phone numbers is redacted from AI prompts. Employees can see and correct their own profile at any time.
7. **Auditability** – All agent actions, tool calls and decisions are logged and kept for 24 months.
