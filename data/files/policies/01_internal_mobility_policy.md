# Internal Mobility Policy (sample policy for TalentFlow demo)

## Purpose
We fill roles with internal talent first. Every open role is advertised on the Internal Talent Marketplace for at least 10 working days before external sourcing begins, unless it is marked Critical by the business unit head.

## Eligibility
- You must have completed at least 12 months in your current role. Employees who are unallocated (on bench) are eligible immediately.
- You must not be on an active Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).
- Your latest performance rating must be 2 or above on the 5-point scale.
- Moves of more than one level upward are treated as promotions and follow the promotion calendar.

## Applying
You may apply to as many as 3 internal roles at the same time. You do not need your manager's approval to apply, but you must inform your manager before the interview stage. Applications are confidential until the interview stage.

## Manager release
If you are allocated to a client project, your current manager must agree a release date. The standard release period is up to 4 weeks after your current project's end date, or 6 weeks for client-billable critical roles. Managers may not block a move for more than one release cycle (90 days).

## Selection
Selections are based on skills, experience, availability and stated career interests. AI-generated shortlists are advisory only; a human hiring manager always makes the final decision and must record a reason for every rejection.
