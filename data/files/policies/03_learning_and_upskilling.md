# Learning & Upskilling Programme (sample)

## Skill adjacency pathways
TalentFlow uses a skills graph to recommend "adjacent" skills you can learn quickly based on what you already know (for example Java → Go, Data Engineering → MLOps, Business Analysis → Product Management). Adjacent-skill learning typically takes 6–12 weeks.

## Learning budget
Every employee has an annual learning budget of USD 1,500 (or local equivalent) and 40 hours of paid learning time. Certifications listed on the internal catalogue are reimbursed in full on passing.

## Stretch assignments
Employees who match 70% or more of a role's required skills may be offered the role as a stretch assignment with a structured 90-day development plan and a mentor.

## Verified skills
Skills can be verified by assessment, project evidence or manager endorsement. Verified skills carry more weight in marketplace matching than self-declared skills.
