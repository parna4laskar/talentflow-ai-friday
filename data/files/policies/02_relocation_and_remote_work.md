# Relocation & Remote Work Guidelines (sample)

## Remote and hybrid roles
Roles marked Remote can be performed from any company site within the same region (APAC, EMEA or AMER). Hybrid roles expect 2–3 days per week at the listed site.

## Relocation support
Where a job posting states relocation is supported, the company provides:
- Economy travel for the employee and immediate family
- Up to 30 days of temporary accommodation
- A one-time relocation allowance equal to one month's base salary (domestic) or two months (international)
- Visa and immigration support for cross-border moves (4–12 weeks lead time)

## Cross-border moves
International moves require the approval of both the sending and receiving country HR leads. Start dates for international moves should allow at least 8 weeks for immigration processing.
